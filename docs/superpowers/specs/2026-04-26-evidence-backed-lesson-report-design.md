# Evidence-Backed Lesson Report — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-04-26
**Status:** Approved
**Author:** Kyle + Claude

---

## Overview

Coachline's lesson report is being redesigned to surface all research-backed coaching signals in a structured, intent-aware layout. The report has three progressive zones on a single scrolling page. Teachers who just want their coaching nudge see Zone 1 and leave. Teachers who want context scroll to Zone 2. Teachers who want all the data expand Zone 3.

The design is grounded in eight bodies of research now implemented or planned in the analysis pipeline. Every metric shown in the UI is backed by peer-reviewed evidence with a known effect size or RCT result.

---

## Report Structure: Three Zones, One Page

The lesson report is a single scrolling page — no tabs, no navigation. Depth increases as you scroll.

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  ZONE 1 — The Coach         │  ← above the fold, primary surface
│  One Move · Highlights ·    │
│  Reflections                │
├─────────────────────────────┤
│  ZONE 2 — The Room          │  ← intent-surfaced; scrolled to
│  3 Focus cards + 3 secondary│
├─────────────────────────────┤
│  ZONE 3 — Full Data         │  ← collapsed by default, expanded on tap
│  All metrics + Transcript   │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

## Zone 1 — The Coach (Hero)

**Purpose:** Every teacher gets exactly one thing to act on. This is Coachline's core value proposition.

### Lesson metadata bar
Displayed at the top of the report:
- Intent badge (e.g. "📋 Discussion") — shows what the teacher said they were trying to do
- Subject ("🔢 Math")
- Topic (e.g. "Adding fractions with unlike denominators") — inferred by the LLM
- Duration

### One Move card
The single most important coaching element. Full-width hero card with:
- **Label:** "⚡ One Move for Your Next Lesson"
- **Title:** Short imperative (e.g. "Add wait time 2 after student responses")
- **Description:** 2-3 sentences connecting the specific lesson data to the move. Must reference actual numbers.
- **Research basis:** One sentence, cites a real researcher and finding (e.g. "Rowe (1986): wait time 2 doubles student reasoning depth")
- **Rehearsal script:** A sample teacher line in italics (e.g. `"Give me a moment to think about what Maya just said..."`)
- Null state: if the pipeline has insufficient signal, the card is omitted entirely — no placeholder

The One Move is generated by the coaching LLM (`report.ts`) and is already weighted toward the teacher's active goal practice area and their lesson intent.

### Highlighted Moments
3–5 moments from the lesson, each with:
- Timestamp (tappable → jumps to that position in the transcript)
- Short title (e.g. "Strong uptake moment")
- 1–2 sentence description referencing what happened and why it matters in the context of intent

### Reflection Prompts
2–3 personalized questions. Must:
- Explicitly reference the lesson intent ("Your intent was *discussion*...")
- Cite specific numbers from this lesson ("...yet 82% of your questions closed without uptake...")
- Not be generic

## Zone 2 — The Room

**Purpose:** Show the 6 research-backed metric cards, with 3 promoted to "Focus" status based on lesson intent.

### Section header
- Label: "The Room"
- Intent lens badge (e.g. "📋 Discussion lens") — explains to the teacher why these cards are highlighted

### Focus cards (3)
Full-size cards with colored top border and "★ FOCUS" badge. Each contains:
- Metric name and icon
- Micro-visualization specific to the metric (bubble chart, bar, turn sequence strip, checklist, etc.)
- Primary number(s)
- A 1-line coaching note that explicitly references the intent (e.g. "For a discussion lesson, aim for 15+ voices. Try cold-call or think-pair-share.")

### Secondary cards (3)
Condensed, dimmed (opacity ~65%). Show:
- Metric name and icon
- Primary number only (no coaching note, no visualization)
- Tappable to expand inline (accordion, same page — no modal, no navigation)

### Intent → Focus card mapping

| Intent | Focus cards (★) | Secondary cards |
|---|---|---|
| `discussion` | Equity of Voice · Dialogue Flow · Student Reasoning | Lesson Launch · Question Quality · Academic Language |
| `inquiry` | Question Quality · Student Reasoning · Dialogue Flow | Lesson Launch · Equity of Voice · Academic Language |
| `direct_instruction` | Lesson Launch · Academic Language · Question Quality | Equity of Voice · Dialogue Flow · Student Reasoning |
| `workshop` | Student Reasoning · Equity of Voice · Academic Language | Dialogue Flow · Lesson Launch · Question Quality |
| `collaborative` | Equity of Voice · Dialogue Flow · Student Reasoning | Lesson Launch · Question Quality · Academic Language |
| `review` | Question Quality · Student Reasoning · Lesson Launch | Equity of Voice · Dialogue Flow · Academic Language |
| `assessment` | Question Quality · Equity of Voice · Lesson Launch | Dialogue Flow · Student Reasoning · Academic Language |
| `null` (not selected) | No focus promotion — all 6 cards shown at equal weight | — |

## The Six New Metric Cards

### 1. Equity of Voice
**Research:** Aguillon et al. (2020, *CBE Life Sciences Education*); Dallimore et al. (2019).
**Signal:** Participation distribution across distinct student speaker IDs from Deepgram diarization.
**Visualization:** Bubble chart — one bubble per student voice, sized by talk-time share. Silent students shown as greyed count.
**Primary numbers:** Unique student voices heard · Gini coefficient · "X of N heard"
**Coaching note (discussion/collaborative):** Compare to class size; benchmark ≤40% of talk from top 3 speakers.
**Backend:** `participationDistribution` already computed in `discourse.ts`. No LLM call.

### 2. Dialogue Flow
**Research:** Mehan (1979); Wells (1999); Demszky et al. (2023 RCT); Murphy et al. (2018, *AERA Journal*).
**Signal:** Ping-pong vs. volleyball index + IRE closure rate.
**Visualization:** Turn sequence strip (colored blocks: blue=teacher, green=student) + three stat chips.
**Primary numbers:** Ping-pong % · Volleyball % · IRE closure rate · Max student chain length
**Coaching note (discussion):** "Benchmark: 20%+ volleyball for discussion intent."
**Backend:** `discoursePatterns` already computed in `discourse.ts`. No LLM call.

### 3. Student Reasoning
**Research:** Bisra et al. (2018, *Educational Psychology Review*) g=0.55.
**Signal:** % of student turns containing causal/evidence language ("because," "since," "the text says," "I think... because," "therefore," etc.)
**Visualization:** Horizontal progress bar with 50% benchmark marker.
**Primary numbers:** Reasoning turn % · Which teacher move types preceded reasoning turns most often
**Coaching note:** Reveal which of the teacher's moves (press, build_on, etc.) actually trigger reasoning language.
**Backend:** Computed via linguistic marker scan on student segments post-analyze (no extra LLM call). Scan for: causal connectors ("because," "so," "therefore," "since," "that means"), evidence markers ("the text says," "in the diagram," "I know because"), claim+hedge ("I think... because"). Flag turn as reasoning if ≥1 marker present. Logic lives in `discourse.ts`. **Not yet built.**

### 4. Lesson Launch
**Research:** Hattie (2009, 2023) d=0.84–0.88 — highest detectable effect size.
**Signal:** Presence of learning intention, success criteria, and relevance hook in the first 5 minutes of teacher speech.
**Visualization:** 3-item checklist with timestamps when each was detected; score 0–3.
**Primary numbers:** Score (0–3) · Timestamp of learning intention if found
**Coaching note:** Missing items listed with suggested language.
**Backend:** New LLM detection needed — scan first 5 min of teacher segments for formulaic intention/criteria language. **Not yet built.**

### 5. Question Quality
**Research:** Herbel-Eisenmann & Breyfogle (2005, *Mathematics Teaching in the Middle School*); Demszky et al. (2023 RCT) — 20% increase in focusing questions with AI feedback.
**Signal:** Secondary classification of "open" questions into funneling (leading toward teacher's predetermined answer) vs. focusing (genuinely inviting student reasoning).
**Visualization:** Split bar (focusing % / funneling %) + benchmark line at 60%.
**Primary numbers:** Focusing % · Funneling % · Sample funneling question
**Coaching note:** "Benchmark: 60%+ focusing. High-performing discussion teachers reach 80%+."
**Backend:** Upgrade to `analyze.ts` — add `funneling` / `focusing` sub-classification to existing `question_open` insights. **Not yet built.**

### 6. Academic Language
**Research:** Beck, McKeown & Kucan (2002, 2013) — Tier 2 vocabulary is the highest lever for academic reading comprehension.
**Signal:** Tier 2/3 word density in teacher speech + rate of in-context definition.
**Visualization:** Tag cloud of Tier 2/3 words sized by frequency; definition rate indicator.
**Primary numbers:** Tier 2 word count · Definition rate · FK readability (already computed)
**Coaching note:** Words used only once flagged as under-exposed; definition rate below 50% flagged.
**Backend:** New computation needed — word-list lookup (Academic Word List, Coxhead 2000) on teacher segments + detection of in-context definitions. **Not yet built.**

## Zone 3 — Full Data

**Purpose:** Complete data for teachers who want it. Collapsed by default behind a "See full analysis ↓" disclosure trigger (native `<details>` element or equivalent accordion component).

### Contents
**Existing metric cards** (unchanged, compact 2-column grid):
- Talk Time (bar chart: teacher/student/group/silence/media)
- Questions (open/closed/focusing/procedural/rhetorical breakdown + DOK 1-4 distribution)
- Wait Time (WT1 avg, WT2 avg, instance counts, top 3 best moments)
- Uptake (count + type breakdown: revoice/build_on/press/connect)
- Praise & Correction (specific/general/correction counts, PBIS ratio)
- Teacher Moves (instruct/explain/question/feedback/manage distribution)
- Vocabulary Grade Level (FK score vs. target grade delta)

**New metric cards — full expanded form:**
All 6 cards from Zone 2 appear here in full detail (complete visualization + all numbers + research citation). The Zone 2 focus cards show a *summary*; Zone 3 shows the *full read*.

**Transcript:**
- Full transcript with audio playback
- Timestamp links from Highlighted Moments land here
- Searchable

## New Goal Practice Areas

The 6 new cards introduce 3 new goal practice areas to the Goals screen:

| Practice Area | `practiceArea` key | Target metric | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equity of Voice | `equity_of_voice` | Gini coefficient | lower = better; target < 0.5 |
| Dialogue Quality | `dialogue_quality` | IRE closure rate | lower = better; target < 0.4 |
| Lesson Clarity | `lesson_clarity` | Lesson Launch score | higher = better; target 3/3 |

These join the existing 8 practice areas in `PRACTICE_AREAS` constant and the `computeGoalMetric` switch in `orchestrator.ts`.

## Success Criteria

- A teacher who opens their report on mobile immediately sees the One Move and two reflection prompts without scrolling
- The intent badge in Zone 2 is self-explanatory — the teacher understands *why* certain cards are highlighted without reading documentation
- A teacher whose lesson intent was "discussion" sees Equity of Voice, Dialogue Flow, and Student Reasoning as focus cards — not the same teacher on a "direct instruction" day
- Zone 3 is invisible to teachers who don't want it; visible and complete for those who do
- All 6 new cards cite a research basis inline
